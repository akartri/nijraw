# Community conduct

The NijRAW community welcomes photographers and contributors of all experience levels. Be respectful, constructive, and patient with questions from beginners.

- Critique ideas, code, and outcomes—not people.
- Do not harass, threaten, discriminate, or share private information without permission.
- Keep bug reports factual and avoid publishing other people's RAW files, faces, or metadata.
- Maintainers may moderate conversations and reject contributions that violate these expectations.

For conduct concerns, contact **contact@akartri.com**, as listed by the application. Share only the information you are comfortable providing.
